Undergraduate Thesis Research

More Than Supervision: Accountability-Driven Coach Agent for Mitigating Academic Procrastination

An HCI research project translating real accountability coaching practices into an LLM-based conversational agent, then evaluating the prototype through a mixed-method user study.

24 stakeholder interviews: 12 coaches and 12 clients
N = 36 4-week randomized mixed-method evaluation
17.71% within-group PASS reduction in the AI coach condition

Research Questions

RQ1 How do accountability coaches and clients describe mechanisms that support academic self-regulation?
RQ2 How can these mechanisms be translated into a stateful LLM-driven conversational coach?

Human-Centered Research Pipeline

1Fieldwork

3Prototype
Implemented coordinator, modules, memory, routing, and adaptation.
4Evaluation
4-week mixed-method study with PASS outcomes and interviews.

Accountability Coaching Service Pattern

System Architecture

The prototype turns the service pattern into a stateful conversational system: user signals update shared context, a coordinator routes intent, and specialized modules generate bounded coaching support.

Qualitative Mechanism Findings

Judgment-Free Reduced social-evaluation anxiety and made honest check-ins easier.
Highly Available Supported immediate help when motivation and task clarity dropped.
Standardized Produced consistent planning and follow-up across repeated turns.
Complementary Human coaching remained stronger for deep empathy and social accountability.
